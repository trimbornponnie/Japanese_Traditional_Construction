# Chapter 18 — Glossary (用語集 / yōgoshū)

**Scope.** This glossary collects the Japanese technical vocabulary used in Chapters 01–17 of this rulebook — 1018 entries covering joints (tsugite, shiguchi) and their parts, pins, wedges and hardware, frame, roof, eave and bracket members, tools and workshop terms, marking and kikujutsu geometry, units and modules, timber and its defects, building types and styles, finishes and fittings, ceremonies, repair practice, and the institutions, laws and manuals cited in the book. Personal and place names are omitted unless they name a technical tradition (e.g. *Heinouchi*, *Kōra*) or a manual (e.g. *Shōmei*).

**How to use it.**

- **Order.** Entries are alphabetical by rōmaji, ignoring macrons, hyphens, spaces and capitals (so *ōbiki* files under O, *kyō-ma* sorts as "kyoma"). Homographs are numbered, e.g. **moya** (1) 母屋 and **moya** (2) 身舎.
- **Rōmaji.** Modified Hepburn with macrons (ō, ū), as in the Style Guide. The headword is the spelling used most often in the book; other spellings that occur in the chapters (hyphenated or run-together compounds, rendaku voicing, alternative readings) are listed as "also …". A full list of variants, with the chapters where they occur, is in Appendix B.
- **日本語.** The usual writing in the book; alternative writings are separated by " / " (e.g. 枘 / ほぞ).
- **Meaning.** A short practitioner's definition consistent with how the chapters use the term. Where the book uses a term in two senses, both are given, numbered. Definitions are summaries; rules, proportions and sources are in the chapters.
- **Ch.** The chapters where the term is treated in depth (at most four, listed in chapter order), in the form used for cross-references elsewhere (→ Ch. 07). Many terms also occur in passing in other chapters.
- **Appendix A** lets a reader who sees a term on a Japanese drawing find it from its characters; **Appendix B** lists spelling variants for editors.
